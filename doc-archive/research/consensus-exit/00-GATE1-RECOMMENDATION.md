## 1. Executive Summary — is this a sound experiment, and under what conditions?

**A weighted-consensus exit core is a SOUND ENGINEERING EXPERIMENT and an UNSOUND STATISTICAL CLAIM — and those are the same fact viewed from two sides.** All three research tracks converge, independently and from different literatures, on one conclusion: at Planet Stopper's data scale (~4 usable validation days per fold; ~5-15 independent regime units in the whole live + historical record), **a tunable-weight exit core cannot be statistically validated, cannot be for the foreseeable operating life of the product, and is therefore inherently a paper-trade OBSERVATION instrument rather than a proof-of-improvement instrument.** This is not a power problem patience fixes; it is the *same structural data wall the project already hit twice and walled off* (the rejected EUT+CVaR migration and the rejected CVaR-divergence detector), now applied to weight selection. The "harden, don't migrate" council verdict transfers with full force, because **this experiment IS an exit-core migration** (prior-researcher §0, §4.1; cross-ref `project_eut_cvar_migration_council_verdict`).

> **THE HEADLINE TENSION the user must confront, stated plainly and up front.** The design exposes **6 tunable knobs** (4 criterion weights + 1 threshold + 1 N-confirms; prior-researcher §3.1 firmed, engine-integrator §1a confirmed from code). The vision is "expose all the knobs, let the data drive them." **The research says the data can honestly drive ≈ ONE of those six knobs; the other five stay at their priors.** This follows from two independent counts that agree: prior-researcher's ~5-15 independent *regime* units vs LdP's ≤1-param-per-independent-observation ceiling (§3), and tuning-methodologist's finding that an honest permission gate on a ~4-day fold mostly outputs "stay on prior" (Finding 1.6). **This does NOT kill the experiment** — because a weight that stays at its prior because the data never earned the move is the *correct* behavior, and the design collapses by degeneracy to a sound fixed-prior baseline the forecast-combination puzzle says is hard to beat anyway (§4.3). But the user must understand, before signing Gate-1, that **the adaptivity they imagine is mostly not earnable on this data**, and decide with that in view — not discover it after a multi-cycle build.

The experiment is nonetheless *sound to run* under three hard conditions, all of which the research establishes as non-negotiable:

1. **It is framed and reported as an observation experiment, never as validated alpha.** The honest gate's *expected output at this data scale is "stay on the prior."** If the gate is honestly calibrated it will almost never grant a weight a move — exactly as the existing autotuner falls back to prior ~78% of the time (prior-researcher §4.3, citing pillar3 §4). The single most dangerous failure mode is loosening the gate so the experiment "feels alive" — that re-imports, *on the live exit path*, the precise overfitting the BHY haircut exists to prevent (prior-researcher §4.3 Option B; tuning-methodologist Finding 1.6, Interpretation §1).

2. **H-1 is fixed first.** The permission-to-tune gate the experiment depends on *is* the existing FDR haircut, and that haircut is **currently mis-wired** — it scores the wrong t-statistic (Sortino) on the canonical CRRA path, LIVE in production (`autotuner.py:1251` hardcodes `compute_sortino_tstat`, ignoring its `tstat_fn` parameter; audit H-1; tuning-methodologist Finding 4.1-4.4; engine-integrator §6). A consensus core built on today's haircut would calibrate every weight-move decision against a sampling distribution unrelated to the consensus objective — "laundering noise as signal" per-weight (tuning-methodologist Finding 4.4). **This is a hard prerequisite, not a parallel nicety, and it is a live defect worth fixing regardless of this experiment.**

3. **The design is pooled and prior-anchored, not free-weight per-symphony.** Free per-symphony weights are *categorically un-tunable* at ~4 days/symphony (tuning-methodologist Finding 2.2). The math forces complete-to-strong-partial pooling, and the warm-start prior must be equal/theory-shaped weights with the burden of proof on any deviation — because the forecast-combination puzzle establishes (decades, heavily replicated) that *estimated* weights underperform fixed equal weights out-of-sample on small samples (prior-researcher §2.1; tuning-methodologist Finding 3.0/3.2). **Critical "two n's" caveat (tuning-methodologist's corrected Finding 2.2):** pooling multiplies the *observation* count backing each weight by ~the symphony count, but it does **NOT** multiply the independent *regime* count — and significance must be read against regimes, not autocorrelated days. The t-stat's √T legitimately uses the day count (a code fact), but the day-vs-regime gap is itself a *source of false permission*; a naïve pooled t over correlated symphony-days overstates effective T and needs a clustered/block SE. So pooling is the right and only powered estimator, yet the honest move-budget stays ≈ 1 knob because the binding constraint is regimes, not raw observations.

**Bottom line for §1:** Sound to *run* as a hardened, pooled, prior-anchored, H-1-fixed, honestly-gated **observation experiment** whose expected behavior is to reproduce today's engine while logging would-be weight moves. Unsound the moment it is described as, or operated to produce, a *validated* alpha improvement. The design's safety and its un-validatability are the same property (prior-researcher §4.3: "safe-by-degeneracy").

## 2. The Recommended Design (the sweet spot)

This is the design the research points to as the honest sweet spot. Section 5 frames the bracketing options (minimal / fuller) around it; this is the recommended middle and the rationale.

### 2.1 Dimensionality & pooling — the sweet spot
- **Voter set: the 4 wired flags only** (`is_trailing_stop_hit`, `is_tp_hit`, `is_vwap_broken`, `is_vwap_bleed_broken`) that `resolve_trigger_priority` arbitrates today (`math_engine.py:836-859`). This gives a **firm canonical free-parameter count of 6 tunables: 4 criterion weights + 1 threshold θ + 1 N-confirms** (~5 effective after the sum/scale constraint removes ~1 DoF) — confirmed against code by engine-integrator (§1a: the "6 layers" are upstream scalars feeding ONE trailing-stop flag; MC is a gate/veto, not a flag) and firmed by prior-researcher (§3.1, lead with 6). **Do NOT promote upstream sub-signals (MC gate, parabolic ratchet, breakeven lock) to weighted voters** — prior-researcher §2.3 shows each is a category mismatch: MC is a *veto/permission* layer (promoting it inverts its role), breakeven is a one-way latch (not expressible as a linear-score weight without breaking its semantics, OQ-P5), and the ratchet is uncalibrated folklore. The 7-voter branch is a *more-invasive redesign the current wiring does not support without restructuring upstream primitives* (engine-integrator §1a), and it inflates the count to ~9-16 (prior-researcher §3.1) — an alternative, not the default. Either way the honest move-budget is ~1 knob (§3).
- **Pooling: one shared (pooled) weight vector across all symphonies — complete pooling.** Free per-symphony weights are ruled out by the data wall (tuning-methodologist Finding 2.2-2.3). Complete pooling multiplies the *observation* count backing each weight by ~the symphony count (NOT the independent regime count — see §1 "two n's" caveat) and is the simplest honest design; a hierarchical partial-pooling layer collapses to near-complete-pooling anyway at T≈4 while adding hard-to-estimate variance components (tuning-methodologist Finding 2.3). **Caveat carried forward (do not bury):** pooling assumes the symphonies are *exchangeable* — if they are structurally heterogeneous, pooling trades variance for bias, and whether the operator's symphonies are exchangeable enough is an *unsettled empirical question* (tuning-methodologist Finding 2.4, OQ-2). This bounds how much pooling is honest and is an open question for the user (§6).

### 2.2 Warm-start priors (per criterion)
The defensible prior is **equal weights as the baseline, with exactly two theory-justified deviations** (prior-researcher §2.1-2.2). Anything more granular is "a guess dressed as a prior" (prior-researcher §2.2):

| Criterion | Warm-start prior | Basis (graded) |
|---|---|---|
| **Trailing Stop** (drawdown truncation) | **Above equal** — the one criterion that earns an above-baseline weight | Mechanical left-tail truncation needs no regime assumption; the regime-independent leg per Kaminski-Lo. `[Theoretical, sound]` |
| **VWAP Bleed Cut** | Equal | Distinct slow-erosion mode; reasonable engineering, no clean academic prior. `[Folklore — moderate]` |
| **Take-Profit** | Equal | Regime-conditional; **no first-principles prior for its priority over Bleed Cut** (audit OQ-1, off-branch H2 doc). `[Backtest, regime-conditional]` |
| **VWAP Breakdown** (as an EXIT) | **Below equal** — the literature leans *against* it | Below-VWAP price empirically mean-reverts *up*; pure VWAP-crossover signals score *below the 5% chance line*; using it as an exit runs opposite the documented edge (audit I-1(c); prior-researcher §2.2). `[Backtest]` against |

> **Honesty flag the user must see:** a "research-derived differentiated prior" that *up*-weighted VWAP-Breakdown would be citing the literature against its own grain — the exact citation-misuse the audit already flagged (I-1). The only defensible differentiation is **trailing stop ≥ others; VWAP-Breakdown ≤ others; everything else ≈ equal.**

- **Prior storage / honesty seam:** PRIOR is stored as a `spec_facet` with `freeze_discipline = THEORY`/`STYLIZED_FACT` on a NEW frozen content-hashed bundle (NOT a mutation of the existing frozen THEORY bundle); the TUNED current value lives in per-symphony params (effective `BACKTEST_SELECTION` provenance once moved). This makes "tuned vs still on prior" visible with **zero new producer code**: Spec Critic already BREACHes on `BACKTEST_SELECTION` (`spec_critic.py:9-13`) and Overfitting Conscience already tracks the added DoF; both surface to the read-only `/ai-advisor` route (`app.py:2198-2224`) (engine-integrator §4, §1d). *(Storage split is engine-integrator's defensible default; flagged to prior-researcher as an open alignment — non-blocking.)*

### 2.3 The permission-to-tune gate + prior-fallback
The gate is a **re-targeting of the existing BHY/Yekutieli FDR haircut** from "which whole param-set wins" to "has this weight earned a move off its prior" (tuning-methodologist Finding 1.1). It already treats each candidate as a hypothesis whose bar rises with the family size via `N_effective = n_optuna + S` (`autotuner.py:761-811`); each weight permitted to float is one more searched dimension and must enter that accounting (Finding 1.3). **Gate placement (resolved between tuning-methodologist and engine-integrator):** the permission gate is **haircut-internal** — it lives inside `_haircut_select` (`autotuner.py:1184-1272`) + `compute_n_effective` (`:761-811`) + the call-site wiring (`:1953-1977`); the OOS AI/fallback/default cascade (`:2090-2107`) is **UNCHANGED** (engine-integrator §1c). So the gate's engineering blast radius is the haircut machinery, not the cascade.

> **THE HINGE between "honestly tunable" and "overfitting trap" (engine-integrator's load-bearing coupling, make explicit).** Each of the 6 new tunables added to the Optuna search space (4 weights + threshold + N-confirms) **MUST be charged into `compute_n_effective`**, or the gate silently under-corrects. Done right, the 6-tunable widening *raises* the significance bar (more hypotheses → stricter BHY) — which is exactly why honest tuning mostly outputs "stay on prior." Done wrong (search widened but `N_effective` not updated), it is **silent overfitting on a ~4-day fold**. This single accounting decision is the dividing line between the safe-by-degeneracy design and a live-path overfitting hazard, and it must be a Gate-2 acceptance criterion.

- **Encoding (recommended): continuous L1-sparse shrinkage toward the prior, on a Dirichlet/simplex-constrained pooled weight vector, gated by the H-1-fixed one-sample t-stat.** L1-toward-prior produces exact snap-backs (weight == prior for sub-threshold evidence) and an interpretable "k of K weights actually moved" active-set count — which is exactly the effective DoF to charge into `N_effective` (tuning-methodologist Finding 3.3, Interpretation §3). The Dirichlet layer enforces the sum-to-threshold + non-negativity the consensus design requires, with one concentration parameter capping total deviation from the prior (Finding 3.2). This keeps effective DoF equal to the number of weights that *earned* a move, not the number that *could*.
- **Prior-fallback in practice:** at T≈4, the honest gate grants permission *rarely*; the default output is prior-persistence (Finding 1.6). That is the gate working correctly. **Recommended calibration:** the Harvey-Liu 2020 double-bootstrap misses-to-false-discoveries hurdle is the literature's name for the experiment's asymmetric "Type-II-tolerant / Type-I-averse" policy (Finding 1.5) — adopting it lets the asymmetry be an explicit, deliberate dial rather than whatever `q=0.05` implies. This is an enhancement, not a prerequisite; the 2015 single-FDR-q gate is an acceptable status-quo starting point.

### 2.4 The N-confirms debounce
- **Placement:** one new transient integer counter `consensus_confirm_count` in the flat `bot_state[symphony_id]` dict, sibling to `below_stop_count`, threaded through the pure `resolve_consensus_exit` exactly like the existing per-criterion confirm counters and persisted by the existing terminal `save_state` — **no new I/O, no new lock, Architecture-Constraint-1 clean** (engine-integrator §3). The replay mirror carries the identical counter for free.
- **Load-bearing design decision the user must make (do not let it pass silently):** stacking a consensus N-confirms debounce *on top of* the existing per-criterion confirms (trailing stop already needs 3 ticks, VWAP 3, TP 2) pushes the design *further toward under-protection* — the audit already finds the current design biased toward fail-to-exit, not over-exit (audit H-3; engine-integrator §3 subtlety). The choice is between (a) the consensus debounce *replacing* per-criterion confirms or (b) *stacking on top* — and collapsing layered confirms requires proving equivalence (engine-integrator §3, citing the risk-engine-specialist charter). **This is a Gate-1 WHAT decision** because it changes the engine's exit aggressiveness. Recommended default: stack lightly (N small, e.g. consensus confirmed on 1-2 ticks) and treat the per-criterion confirms as the primary debounce, but flag for explicit user choice (§6).

## 3. Hard Dependencies (the two prerequisite fixes, with file:line)

These are not part of the consensus design — they are pre-existing defects the consensus design *cannot honestly sit on top of*. Both are independently confirmed in the prior audit.

### 3.1 H-1 — the gate statistic must be corrected before any weight-move decision is trustworthy
**What it is today:** `_haircut_select` (`autotuner.py:1184-1272`) accepts a `tstat_fn` parameter and its docstring says swapping it is "the ONLY permitted change" (`:1203-1207`); the call site correctly routes `tstat_fn=compute_crra_eu_tstat` for the canonical CRRA bundle (`:1953-1954`, `:1976-1977`); **but the loop hardcodes `compute_sortino_tstat(series, seed=trial_idx)` (`:1251`) and never calls `tstat_fn`**, computing then discarding `_crra_gamma` (`:1243`). Runtime-confirmed LIVE on the canonical THEORY-bundle path (audit H-1; tuning-methodologist Finding 4.1; engine-integrator §6).

**What it must BECOME for a weighted-consensus objective** (tuning-methodologist Finding 4.2-4.3):
1. `_haircut_select` must actually call its `tstat_fn` inside the loop (literal fix to `:1251`), resolving the signature mismatch (`compute_sortino_tstat(returns, seed)` vs the no-seed mean-valued form) — this is the minimal fix and is a live-defect fix *independent of the experiment*.
2. The consensus objective is a **mean-valued functional** (a weighted sum/aggregate per day), NOT a ratio (Sharpe/Sortino). Therefore the gate statistic MUST be the **one-sample t-statistic of the per-day consensus objective series**, `mean(series)/(sd(series)/√T)` — `compute_crra_eu_tstat`'s form (`:520-553`) is reusable; what differs is the input transform.
3. **The per-day "score" series must be pinned** (raw consensus score vs realized utility vs P&L). *This choice IS the objective definition — it is a Gate-1 decision, not an implementation detail* (tuning-methodologist Finding 4.3 step 2, OQ-1). It is in §6.
4. Build the consensus per-day series on the *same purged validation fold* (PURGE_DAYS=20, EMBARGO_DAYS=1; `:1799-1802`), or it silently reintroduces look-ahead (tuning-methodologist Statistical Validity §).

### 3.2 H-3 — graceful degradation: consensus IMPROVES it but does NOT close it
**The precise, non-overstated finding** (engine-integrator §5, cross-ref audit H-3):
- **What consensus genuinely fixes (the scoring layer):** under a weighted sum, a criterion whose signal is unavailable contributes *zero weight* while the others sum toward the threshold — strictly better than today's silent rung-drop in the fixed ladder — **but ONLY IF weight floors guarantee the MC-independent criteria (VWAP Breakdown System A + VWAP Bleed System B, `math_engine.py:744-787`) can sum to ≥ threshold on their own.** Setting those floors is a Gate-1/design requirement.
- **What consensus does NOT fix (the arming layer — the actual H-3):** H-3's root cause is *upstream* of the resolver, at the arming gate `alpha_bot_execution.py:1292-1303`, where the only `armed=True` path requires `mc_available` (`:1294`), and `compute_exit_confirmation` returns early `(count, False)` when `(not armed)` (`math_engine.py:503-504`). When MC is *permanently* absent, the trailing-stop criterion never even produces a `True` flag to feed the consensus scorer — it is gated out one layer above where consensus lives.

> **HARD INSTRUCTION for any downstream Gate-1 narrative:** do NOT claim "consensus fixes H-3." The honest statement is: **consensus improves graceful degradation at the signal-combination layer, but the documented H-3 fail-to-arm defect is a separate upstream arming-gate fix that the consensus work must either depend on or bundle, and weight floors must guarantee MC-independent solo-fire regardless** (engine-integrator §5). The audit's recommended H-3 fix-shape (a no-MC fail-safe arming path) interacts favorably: fix H-3, and the trailing-stop flag can fire under no-MC, and *then* consensus combines it gracefully with VWAP.

### 3.3 A possible third co-prerequisite — CPCV (flagged, not asserted)
If the experiment wants weights to *actually move* (not merely sit on prior), single-path walk-forward at T≈4 likely cannot power K per-weight tests; López de Prado's combinatorial purged CV (named-but-unimplemented in the code, `autotuner.py:368-370`) may be a co-prerequisite alongside H-1 (tuning-methodologist Statistical Validity §, OQ-4; prior-researcher §4.4). **Graded `[interpretation, directly grounded in the audit's CPCV finding]`** — I carry it as a flagged dependency for the user, not a settled fact. Note CPCV improves *selection-variance accounting*; it does **not** manufacture independent regimes that do not exist (prior-researcher §4.4).

### 3.4 The VWAP weight-FLOOR — a binding SAFETY constraint that overrides the gate, and the deeper "wrong voter basis" warning
This is a constraint, surfaced by both prior-researcher (new §2.4) and tuning-methodologist (Finding 1.4c), that the build must honor and that exposes a genuine design tension the user should weigh.

- **The binding constraint (a safety floor on the optimizer's feasible region, NOT a tuning knob):** the two MC-independent flags — VWAP Breakdown (System A) + VWAP Bleed Cut (System B) — must carry weight priors that **sum to ≥ threshold θ on their own**, and **the permission gate must NOT tune those weights below that floor regardless of statistical significance** (tuning-methodologist Finding 1.4c). Otherwise a permanent MC outage starves the consensus score below θ and the combination layer re-imports an H-3-flavored fail-closed in the exact thin-MC regime the vision says matters most. This is the one place a non-statistical PROTECTION constraint correctly overrides the gate — it is a constraint on the optimizer's feasible region, not a parameter it may move.
- **The CONFLICT the user must see (prior-researcher §2.4, load-bearing):** the fail-safe floor pushes VWAP weight **UP**, but prior-researcher's literature finding pushes VWAP-Breakdown-as-exit **DOWN** (below-VWAP price mean-reverts up; crossover signals score below the 5% chance line). The fail-safe forces non-trivial weight onto exactly the criterion the literature distrusts most. *Honest reconciliation (an option, not a directive):* let **Bleed Cut carry more of the floor than Breakdown** — Bleed has no negative literature prior, Breakdown does.
- **The deeper warning (prior-researcher §2.4, carried prominently):** that this conflict exists at all is *evidence the 4 wired flags are a poor voter BASIS for a weighted-magnitude scheme* — **they were designed for PRIORITY/ordering (fastest-hard-cut-first), not for MAGNITUDE/summation.** A weighted sum asks "how much should each contribute," a question the flags were never engineered to answer. This is the strongest single argument that even Option B's expressiveness is partly illusory: tuning magnitudes over a basis built for ordering may not be a well-posed problem. It does not block the experiment, but it sharpens why the recommended posture is observation-not-validation, and it is an open question for the user (§6).

## 4. CANDID Validatability Statement (what this can and cannot EVER prove)

This is the section the user most needs to read before signing Gate-1. It is stated without hedging.

**What the experiment CAN do:**
- Produce a **safe re-parameterization** of today's exit engine. Because the honest gate degenerates to prior-persistence, the live behavior in the foreseeable future is ≈ the current fixed-priority engine expressed as a weighted sum (prior-researcher §4.3 "safe-by-degeneracy"; tuning-methodologist Interpretation §1).
- Produce a **structured observation log** of would-be weight moves — "the engine would have moved weight X off its theory anchor under a 4-day fold, but the gate did/did not permit it." This is a genuine *governance/diagnostic* artifact that surfaces to `/ai-advisor` via the existing advisors with no new producer (engine-integrator §4).
- **Improve graceful degradation at the signal-combination layer** (the zero-weight-for-missing-signal property, §3.2) — a real, citable engineering improvement over the silent rung-drop, conditional on weight floors and bundled with the H-3 arming fix.
- Serve as the substrate for **operator paper-trade evaluation** — the path the project's own README already prescribes (operator runs paper mode and judges fit), because the engine cannot prove value from history at this data scale.

**What the experiment CANNOT do — ever, at this data scale:**
- **It cannot statistically prove the tuned weights beat the priors out-of-sample.** Three independent walls converge (prior-researcher §4.2): (1) the *estimation-error wall* — on samples this small, estimated weights are *expected* to underperform fixed equal weights OOS (forecast-combination puzzle, heavily replicated `[Theoretical+Backtest, High]`); (2) the *degrees-of-freedom wall* — ~5-15 independent regime units vs the design's 6 canonical knobs (9-16 in the invasive 7-voter variant) violates LdP's 1-param-per-observation ceiling several-fold; the honest move-budget is ≈ 1 knob `[Methodological, High]`; (3) the *regime wall* — the upside-capture legs are regime-conditional and the operator's measured intraday regime is the *unfavorable* (mean-reverting) one (pooled lag-1 AC = −0.036, pillar3) `[Backtest/Empirical, High]`.
- **It cannot escape the wall by reframing.** The project already proved twice that reframing (CVaR-as-trigger → CVaR-as-divergence-detector) only *relocates* the data wall, it does not buy new validation budget (`project_cvar_divergence_validation_wall`). Weight-tuning is the same wall applied to weights.
- **It cannot be made "adaptive AND validatable" at this scale.** There is no third option: an honest gate rarely moves (safe but mostly reproduces today's engine); a loose gate moves often (visible adaptivity, negative expected OOS value, re-imported overfitting on the live path) (prior-researcher §4.3).

> **The one-sentence honest verdict:** *This is a paper-trade observation experiment that, run honestly, will mostly reproduce the current engine while logging the weight moves it declined to make — and that degeneracy is precisely what makes it safe. Anyone who needs to see weights move to feel the experiment "worked" will be tempted to break the only thing that keeps it honest.*

## 5. Options (2-3 concrete shapes) + Recommendation

All three options assume the two hard dependencies (§3.1 H-1, §3.2 H-3) are addressed; none is honest without them. All are new codepaths → Agent-Teams TDD; all are a coordinated 4-file production change (`math_engine.py` + `alpha_bot_execution.py` + `autotuner.py` + DB schema/defaults) with a large parity-test surface — **multi-cycle, not math_engine-local** (engine-integrator §2, §7).

### Option A — Minimal: pooled, prior-anchored, **fixed** weights + N-confirms (no live tuning)
- **What:** Replace the fixed-priority ladder with a pooled weighted-consensus scorer using the §2.2 warm-start priors as *fixed* weights (equal-ish, trailing-stop-up, VWAP-Breakdown-down). Add the tunable threshold + N-confirms debounce. **No permission-to-tune gate at all** — weights never move.
- **Data requirement:** none beyond what exists; nothing is estimated.
- **Tradeoffs:** Captures the *only* literature-supported design (fixed/equal weights beat estimated weights OOS, §2.1) and the graceful-degradation improvement (§3.2) with the *smallest* risk surface. Loses the "tunes as data accrues" aspiration entirely. Honest by construction — there is no gate to loosen.
- **Risk:** Lowest. The main risk is purely the engineering blast radius (parity-pinned dual-site swap) and the N-confirms under-protection interaction (§2.4).

### Option B — Recommended Middle: pooled, prior-anchored weights + **honest permission-to-tune gate** (shared vector)
- **What:** Option A **plus** the §2.3 permission gate (L1-sparse shrinkage to prior on a Dirichlet/simplex pooled vector, H-1-fixed one-sample t-stat, charging `N_effective` per weight that moves). Weights *may* move off prior, pooled across all symphonies, but only when statistically earned — which at this data scale is *rare* (§4).
- **Data requirement:** the gate consumes the existing ~4-day fold; honestly, it will mostly decline to move. To *actually* move weights, CPCV (§3.3) is likely a co-prerequisite.
- **Tradeoffs:** Delivers the full stated experiment (warm-start + permission-based tuning + prior-fallback) while remaining honest *because* the gate degenerates to prior-persistence (§4.3). Adds the gate machinery and the H-1 dependency becomes load-bearing (not just a good-hygiene fix). Produces the observation-log governance artifact (§4).
- **Risk:** Medium, and *entirely concentrated in gate calibration* — a loosely-calibrated gate is the one way this option becomes dishonest. The 2020 asymmetric hurdle (§2.3) is the recommended guardrail.

### Option C — Fuller: **per-symphony** permission-tuned weights + threshold + N
- **What:** Option B but with per-symphony weight deviations (partial pooling: shared prior + shrunk per-symphony offsets).
- **Data requirement:** far higher — per-symphony n is ~4 days, *below* the effective-observations-per-group threshold, so the partial-pooling model *collapses to near-complete-pooling anyway* (tuning-methodologist Finding 2.2-2.3) while adding hard-to-estimate variance components and a cross-symphony correlation hazard (a naïve pooled t over correlated symphony-days overstates effective T — needs a clustered/block SE; Finding 2.4, Statistical Validity §).
- **Tradeoffs:** Most expressive on paper; in practice buys almost nothing over Option B at this n while adding estimable nuisance parameters and a new look-ahead-adjacent hazard. Worth it *only if/when* per-symphony n grows past the threshold.
- **Risk:** Highest, for the least marginal benefit at the current data scale.

### RECOMMENDATION
**Option B, sequenced behind the H-1 and H-3 fixes, framed explicitly as an observation experiment.** Rationale:
- Option B is the *full* experiment the user described, and it is honest *precisely because* the gate's expected behavior is prior-persistence — the safety and the un-validatability are the same property (§4). It is the design that composes the project's existing NN1/`N_effective`/BHY discipline with the permission-to-tune philosophy *without inventing new machinery* (tuning-methodologist Interpretation §3).
- Option A is the safe fallback if the user wants the graceful-degradation + transparency benefits *without* taking on the gate-calibration risk surface at all. It is a legitimate, fully-honest choice — and it is the natural *first cycle* even if the user wants Option B eventually (build the pooled fixed-weight scorer + N-confirms first; add the gate second).
- Option C is **not recommended** at the current data scale — it costs the most and buys the least, and the math forces it to collapse toward Option B regardless.

> **Sequencing the recommendation implies (for Gate-2, not decided here):** (1) fix H-1 (live defect, independent value); (2) fix/bundle H-3 arming path + set MC-independent weight floors; (3) Option A scorer (fixed pooled weights + N-confirms, parity-pinned dual-site); (4) Option B gate on top. ## 6. Open Questions for the User to Decide at Gate-1

These are genuine WHAT decisions that research cannot settle — they need the user.

1. **Objective definition (load-bearing).** What is the per-day "score" series the gate's t-statistic is computed over — raw weighted consensus score, realized CRRA utility, or realized P&L? *This choice IS the objective definition and determines the H-1 consensus statistic* (tuning-methodologist Finding 4.3, OQ-1). Cannot be resolved by research.
2. **N-confirms layering.** Does the consensus N-confirms debounce *replace* the per-criterion confirms or *stack on top* of them? Stacking pushes further toward under-protection; collapsing requires proving equivalence (§2.4; engine-integrator §3). This changes the engine's exit aggressiveness — a WHAT decision.
3. **Honesty contract / acceptance of degeneracy.** Does the user accept, up front, that the honest gate's *expected* behavior is "stay on prior" and that the deliverable is a safe re-parameterization + observation log, NOT a validated alpha improvement (§4)? If the user expects to *see* weights move, that expectation must be confronted now — satisfying it is the one way to break the experiment's honesty.
4. **Voter set.** Confirm the 4-flag voter set (recommended) vs promoting sub-signals (not recommended; category mismatches + DoF inflation) (§2.1; prior-researcher OQ-P1).
5. **Symphony exchangeability / pooling level.** Are the operator's symphonies similar enough to share one weight vector? This bounds how much pooling is honest and is an unsettled empirical question (§2.1 caveat; tuning-methodologist Finding 2.4). Defaulting to complete pooling (Option B) is defensible; the user should confirm the symphonies aren't so heterogeneous that pooling injects meaningful bias.
6. **CPCV appetite.** If the user wants weights to actually move (not just be logged as declined), is the team willing to take on CPCV as a co-prerequisite (§3.3)? If not, Option B is honest but will behave ≈ Option A in practice.
7. **Gate calibration policy.** Adopt the Harvey-Liu 2020 asymmetric misses-to-false-discoveries hurdle (explicit Type-II-tolerant dial) vs inherit the 2015 single-FDR-q gate (§2.3)? Enhancement, not a prerequisite.
8. **"Wrong voter basis" — is tuning magnitudes over an ordering-built basis well-posed?** The 4 wired flags were designed for PRIORITY/ordering, not MAGNITUDE/summation, and the fail-safe floor forces weight onto exactly the criterion (VWAP-Breakdown) the literature distrusts most (§3.4). Does the user accept tuning weights over this basis as-is, or does this argue for revisiting the voter set / criterion design before (or instead of) building the weighted core? A WHAT-level question about whether the experiment is well-posed, not just how to build it.

> **One item that is NOT an open question but a Gate-2 acceptance criterion (flagged so it is not lost):** the 6 new tunables MUST be charged into `compute_n_effective` (§2.3 hinge). This is non-negotiable for honesty — surfaced here so the user/PM ensures it lands as a build A/C, not an optional optimization.
