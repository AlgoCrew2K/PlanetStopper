{% call glossary("History") %}

Windows: calendar days counted back from the session shown; YTD starts on 1 January and 5Y covers five years.

Exit alpha: an exit's return minus its held return, in pts, which are percentage points.

The time in the note: when the bot freezes each exit's result for the day's record, the same afternoon check the dashboard calls snapshot-time.

Dollar result: the dollars the exits saved or lost against holding, on the same snapshot basis as the dashboard.

Win rate: the share of exits whose exit alpha was above zero.

Wins: exits whose exit alpha was above zero. Avg per exit: the reason's sum of exit alpha divided by its number of exits.

Detail: the exit's exit alpha, in pts.
