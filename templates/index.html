Fleet Correlation Risk

When many symphonies exit on the same signal at once, the fleet effectively behaves as a single concentrated position. This amplifies drawdown risk — losses that would otherwise be spread across uncorrelated strategies compound simultaneously.

Threshold

>50% of the fleet exiting on one shared reason within ~3 minutes triggers this alert.
