Risk metrics

Positive deltas on risk metrics (Sharpe, Sortino, drawdown reduction) mean the bot improved risk-adjusted outcomes vs holding through. Delta is bot minus baseline; drawdown and volatility deltas are in %.

What the terms mean

Max DD: the largest fall from a high to a later low over the window, shown as a size.

Calmar: yearly return divided by the deepest fall; higher is better.

Annualized volatility: how widely daily returns swing, scaled to a year.

Volatility reduction: how much calmer the bot's returns were than holding's, in percentage points.

Total return: the whole window's return, compounded.

Annualized return (CAGR): the yearly rate that compounds to the total return.

Up days: the share of days with a gain.

Stop-justification preconditions

Checks whether each symphony's daily returns behave in the way that makes a trailing stop pay off on average: a day's move tends to carry into the next by more than the day's risk-adjusted return. Not met means this sample does not show that, not that the stop is wrong (Kaminski and Lo, 2014). Replay = simulated if-held daily series from the last autotune/replay run; Shadow = live if-held daily series across all position epochs of the symphony.

What the terms mean

Sample: which daily series was checked, Replay or Shadow, as the note above says.

ρ: lag-1 autocorrelation, how much one day's return tends to carry into the next.

CI: the confidence range that ρ likely sits in.

Daily Sharpe: the average daily return divided by its volatility, not annualized.

N: how many daily returns the sample holds.

Sufficient met: even at the low end of its range, the day-to-day carry beats the daily Sharpe ratio, which is enough to justify the stop on average.

Sufficient likely: the carry beats the daily Sharpe ratio at its central estimate, but its range overlaps it.

Not met: this sample does not show enough carry; that is not proof the stop is wrong, and it says nothing about drawdown protection.

Negative edge: the daily Sharpe ratio is zero or below, so no carry can justify the stop on return grounds.

Insufficient data: too few days to judge either way.

Autotune: the weekly search for better stop settings; its last run leaves the replay series this table reads.
