### AC-2 and AC-3: errors never drawn as data; the stream re-arms and the panel refreshes without it (C2)

The waiting body passes (S-2.9). A failure renders nothing, calls `noteRefreshFailed()` and `renderAsOf()`, and the next 30 s poll is the retry. The sequence check runs before classification, so a superseded error is ignored (S-2.10). `loadState` reads `sentAtMono = performance.now()` immediately before `fetch` and, in the first `.then`, builds the receipt record with `window.receiptTiming(sentAtMono, r)` (RC-3); the applied path passes it to `noteDataReceived` and `updateChromeTicker`.
