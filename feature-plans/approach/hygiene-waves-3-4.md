The split follows the content pins and two straddles found in the code: S-1.1 scans names in `autotuner.py`, `math_engine.py`, `database.py` and `analytics.py` together, and S-4.3 counts JSON parse definitions, which include `_parse_llm_suggestion_row` until AC-1 deletes it. A cycle holding only half of either would fail its own scan until the other merged, so every deletion and every pinned file sits in C1.

- C1, engine, math, database and the flag parse (AC-1, AC-3, AC-4, AC-5, AC-10, S-9.1 to S-9.3). Two RED batches share one implementer, on disjoint test files:
  - Batch A, `quant-test-writer` (project rule: math layer and engine edits; Sensitive money gates): the z-score, all ten flag sites, the dip line, the autotuner seed and n_jobs rows, the `regime_classifier` docstring and every content pin.   - Batch B, `test-writer`: the database and analytics deletions with their test moves, AC-4, the two app comment rows and the unchanged-surface records in `tests/fixtures/hygiene_waves_3_4/app_database/`.
  - GREEN: one `implementer`. The Batch A author is the closer; after GREEN it re-takes the three full-file pins in a test-only commit, only once the scope pins written at RED pass (Q-1). - Review, one round at the end, dispatched by the PM when all three cycle-completes are in, in parallel: one `qa` in verify mode serving the three heads; one `qa` in hidden mode (Sensitive plan; hidden scenarios written after approval); one `code-reviewer` serving the three heads; `quant-code-reviewer` on C1 (math_engine and engine edits); `pipelines-expert` on C2 (`regime_label_job.py`, the scheduler and run-bound tests); `integration-auditor` on C1 and on C2 (cross-surface deletions: database, analytics, autotuner, math and route handlers in C1; the root module and the scheduler-adjacent tests in C2). No `ux-expert` or capturer and no `api-expert` (Items Raised 3). One `doc-gen` at the slice tail, after every reviewer passes every head.
- Isolation follows the project's environment rules: each teammate works in its own worktree off the base below, never in the primary checkout; kickoffs mandate `cd` plus a `git rev-parse --abbrev-ref HEAD` and `git rev-parse HEAD` check and copy `.env` into the worktree. Pytest runs `-n0` on named files only, never the full tree and never `-o addopts=""`. The real account id is never written into a brief, message, commit, test name or log.

Team size: 15 = 1 quant-test-writer + 3 test-writer + 3 implementer + 1 qa + 1 qa (hidden mode) + 1 code-reviewer + 1 quant-code-reviewer + 1 pipelines-expert + 2 integration-auditor + 1 doc-gen
